# Coaching Privacy Guardrails

- Client data is private by default.
- Coach has no direct SELECT permission to journal_entries, daily_checkins, support_contacts, triggers or full history merely because a relationship exists.
- Client-selected items are copied into coach_shared_items.
- Client can revoke a shared item.
- Messages are visible only to coaching relationship/group participants.
- Coaching invitations require the invited email to match the signed-in account.
- Public coach marketplace is disabled until legal/quality review.
- Coach payment processing is disabled in this build.
- Domestic violence, minors, medical/mental-health management and regulated provider use require additional safety/legal review.
