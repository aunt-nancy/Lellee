Privacy

Sensitive recovery information

Lellee is designed around authenticated accounts, private user records, row-level access controls, and user-directed sharing choices. Final launch review must confirm the complete data flow, vendors, retention practices, security program, contractual obligations, and applicable law.

Personalization

Personalization uses information saved in the user's own Lellee account to choose what Lellee surfaces first. Users can turn personalization off. The release candidate does not intentionally enable advertising or advertising-based behavioral profiling.

Recovery Story

Journal entries and guided responses are excluded from Recovery Story unless the user deliberately approves them.

Account controls

The app includes data export, recovery-date history, password recovery, and an account-deletion request workflow. Final operational deletion timelines and identity-verification procedures must be established before launch.
