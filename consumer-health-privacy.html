Sources

Most consumer health data comes directly from you when you create an account, answer a prompt, write an entry, select preferences or save a support/resource item. Limited technical information comes from your browser/device and Lellee's infrastructure.

Uses

Lellee uses consumer health data to provide requested recovery features, maintain private history, personalize content when enabled, send requested reminders, operate security and respond to privacy requests. Lellee's consumer launch policy is not to use private recovery data for targeted advertising.

Sharing

Consumer health data may be processed by vendors acting for Lellee when necessary to provide a requested service. The current vendor categories include authentication/database/server hosting, web hosting, browser push delivery and, once enabled, payment processing. Lellee does not sell consumer health data under the launch policy in this build.

Rights and requests

Depending on the law that applies, a consumer may have rights to confirm collection/sharing/sale, access information, obtain information about recipients, withdraw consent from certain future collection or sharing, delete information, or appeal a denied request. Lellee's Privacy & Data workflow is designed to accept those categories of request broadly rather than making a user prove a state-law threshold first.

How to request

Use the signed-in Privacy & Data area. Lellee may authenticate the request before disclosing or deleting sensitive data.

Sale

Lellee's launch policy is no sale of consumer health data. A future change to that policy would require a separate legal, product and consent review before implementation.

Changes to categories or purposes

Before materially expanding the categories or purposes of consumer health data collection, use or sharing, Lellee should update this notice and obtain any affirmative consent required by applicable law.

Lellee — private, practical recovery support for today and the long view. The consumer service is not an emergency service, licensed treatment program, or substitute for individualized medical or behavioral-health care.
