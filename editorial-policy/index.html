EDITORIAL POLICY

Helpful, careful, transparent recovery information

Lellee's public content is intended to help people understand recovery concepts and find appropriate next steps without pretending to provide individualized clinical care.

People-first content

Pages should answer a real recovery question, add useful context, avoid keyword stuffing, and provide a clear next step. Pages are not published merely to create search traffic.

Health and recovery sources

Health-adjacent claims should be checked against authoritative sources such as the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) and other appropriate public-health or primary sources.

Local resource standards

Location pages are not allowed to invent providers or imply availability. A category-specific local page should remain out of search indexes until it contains enough verified, useful local information to stand on its own.

Sponsorship

Sponsored directory placements must be clearly labeled. Sponsorship may affect visibility in the directory, but does not alter emergency guidance, safety logic, or imply that a resource is clinically appropriate for a particular person.

Corrections and updates

Content should be reviewed when authoritative guidance changes. Resource availability, eligibility, cost and services must be confirmed directly with the resource.

Reference guidance: SAMHSA — About Recovery · SAMHSA — Substance Use Disorder Treatment
