LELLEE LOCAL SEO PUBLISHING GUARDRAIL

City hub pages may be indexed when they provide useful orientation and access to the live Lellee resource search.

City + resource-category pages are currently NOINDEX.

Before changing a category page to indexable:
1. Add at least 5 currently verified local resources.
2. Include verification/review dates.
3. Include meaningful comparison/helpful information, not only names and links.
4. Clearly label paid/sponsored placements.
5. Do not state or imply that sponsorship equals clinical recommendation.
6. Confirm the page is substantially useful without requiring the visitor to click elsewhere.
7. Add the page to sitemap.xml only after it passes review.

This avoids manufacturing thin location pages solely for search traffic.
