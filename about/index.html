ABOUT LELLEE

Support that grows with your life

Lellee is a private, practical companion for recovery, caregiving, housing stability, reentry, employment transitions, independent living and personal growth.

What Lellee is designed to do

Lellee brings together manageable actions, reflection, tools, learning, connection and resources to help you take your next step. Explore support pathways and optional human coaching as your needs change. Within Recovery, Lellee supports multiple recovery pathways rather than treating one pathway as the only legitimate route.

What Lellee is not

Lellee is not a diagnosis, emergency service, medical provider, treatment program or replacement for professional care. The public learning library is educational.

How public content is developed

Public recovery content is written for people first, reviewed against authoritative recovery and treatment guidance, and separated from paid placement. Sponsored directory visibility does not imply clinical appropriateness.

Core recovery stays free

The private app keeps essential guided recovery, Help Me Right Now, core coping tools, Journal, Support and Resources available without a Plus subscription.
