# Lellee Wave 1 — Specialist Review Packet

> **STATUS UPDATE — SUPERSEDED FOR THE CURRENT COACHING-ONLY BETA (2026-09-11).**  
> The current Wave 1 beta offers coaching only. Clinical care, individualized legal advice, housing placement, case management, broad program-specific resource navigation, and emergency services are not offered. Crisis concerns are referred externally to 988 in the U.S.; immediate danger or a medical emergency is directed to 911 or the nearest emergency room.  
> This packet is retained for audit history and future expansion. It is **not a current beta release gate**. Appropriate specialist review must be reintroduced before any of the expanded services below are activated. See `WAVE_1_COACHING_ONLY_SCOPE_2026-09-11.md`.

## Original review rule

This packet was created when Wave 1 contemplated broader safety-sensitive, legal-sensitive, accessibility and privacy routing. It is preserved so the review requirements are not lost if those features return.

For any future expansion into the services described below, the appropriate qualified reviewer should mark: APPROVE / APPROVE WITH CHANGE / BLOCK, and provide correction text when needed.

## Caregiving — future heightened review if broader services are activated

1. **I’m overwhelmed or need a break**
   - Historical route: Support Contact
   - Historical guidance: Reduce the immediate load first. Identify what truly needs attention, what can wait, and whether a trusted person or caregiver resource can help.
   - Future review focus: avoid implying medical triage; appropriate caregiver-burden boundary.

2. **The care situation changed suddenly**
   - Historical route: In-app Guidance
   - Historical guidance: Organize the change, upcoming instructions and follow-up questions. Lellee does not diagnose or change treatment instructions.
   - Future review focus: health-navigation boundary and when professional/emergency care should supersede ordinary planning.

3. **I am worried about immediate safety**
   - Historical route: Emergency Care
   - Historical guidance: If someone is in immediate danger or there is a medical emergency, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Future review focus: emergency wording and non-emergency alternatives if Lellee adds broader care-navigation functionality.

## Returning Home / Reentry — future heightened review if broader services are activated

4. **I do not have a safe place to stay**
   - Historical route: Resource Navigation
   - Historical guidance: Prioritize immediate safe housing and basic needs, then organize follow-up steps.
   - Future review focus: homelessness/safety routing; avoid implying guaranteed placement.

5. **I am worried about a requirement or deadline**
   - Historical route: In-app Guidance
   - Historical guidance: Organize the requirement, date and questions. Lellee provides planning support, not individualized legal advice.
   - Future review focus: legal-information boundary, court/probation/parole deadlines, and escalation language.

6. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Historical route: Emergency Care
   - Historical guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Future review focus: emergency wording if broader safety routing is added.

## Housing Stability — future heightened review if broader services are activated

7. **I may lose my housing soon**
   - Historical route: Resource Navigation
   - Historical guidance: Identify notices and deadlines, gather documents, and connect with qualified housing or legal resources as appropriate.
   - Future review focus: legal-information boundary; eviction deadlines; no individualized legal advice.

8. **I have nowhere safe to stay**
   - Historical route: Resource Navigation
   - Historical guidance: Prioritize immediate shelter and basic-needs resources, then organize longer-term housing follow-up.
   - Future review focus: shelter/resource wording; avoid promising availability.

9. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Historical route: Emergency Care
   - Historical guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Future review focus: emergency wording if broader safety routing is added.

## Building Independence — future accessibility/privacy review for expanded services

10. **An access barrier is stopping me**
   - Historical route: Resource Navigation
   - Historical guidance: Identify the barrier and the accommodation, assistive technology, transportation, advocacy or community-living support that may help.
   - Future review focus: disability-respectful language, autonomy, accessibility and accommodation boundaries.

11. **I need more or less support**
   - Historical route: Support Contact
   - Historical guidance: Review what you want to do yourself, where support is useful, and who you choose to involve.
   - Future review focus: supported decision-making, autonomy, privacy, avoiding paternalistic framing.

12. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Historical route: Emergency Care
   - Historical guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Future review focus: emergency wording and accessibility considerations.

## Cross-program privacy requirements retained for all future expansion

- cross-program sharing remains OFF by default;
- private Journal and private messages are not used unless explicitly shared;
- Trusted People access remains purpose-specific and revocable;
- program setup answers are user-entered and are not interpreted as diagnosis or eligibility findings;
- expanded resource tools must not promise eligibility, housing placement, legal outcomes or clinical results;
- no agent independently diagnoses, prescribes, changes treatment, or makes emergency-disposition decisions.

## Accessibility requirements that still apply now

The current coaching-only beta still requires:
- keyboard-operable controls and visible focus;
- meaningful labels for screen readers;
- no information conveyed by color alone;
- readable scaling at mobile zoom/text enlargement;
- plain language;
- user control over trusted-person involvement and shared information;
- no score that equates independence with doing everything without support.

These current requirements are tested through `wave1-accessibility-qa.html`.

## Future review handling

If Lellee later activates clinical, individualized legal, housing-placement, case-management, broad resource-navigation, or more complex safety-routing features, restore the appropriate specialist review before activation. A future specialist approval still would not itself make a program public; it would satisfy only the relevant review gate.
