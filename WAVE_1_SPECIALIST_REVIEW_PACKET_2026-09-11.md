# Lellee Wave 1 — Specialist Review Packet

Date: 2026-09-11
Purpose: final required human review before controlled beta recruitment.
Status: REVIEW PACKET — no route is published and no beta tester is active.

## Review rule

This review is limited to safety-sensitive, legal-sensitive, accessibility and privacy boundaries. Reviewers are not being asked to redesign the product. Product-owner approval is already recorded for the working copy; this packet asks whether the wording and routing are safe enough for a small controlled beta.

For each item, reviewer should mark: APPROVE / APPROVE WITH CHANGE / BLOCK, and provide correction text when needed.

## Caregiving — heightened review

1. **I’m overwhelmed or need a break**
   - Urgency: Elevated
   - Route: Support Contact
   - Working guidance: Reduce the immediate load first. Identify what truly needs attention, what can wait, and whether a trusted person or caregiver resource can help.
   - Review focus: avoid implying medical triage; appropriate caregiver-burden boundary.

2. **The care situation changed suddenly**
   - Urgency: Elevated
   - Route: In-app Guidance
   - Working guidance: Organize the change, upcoming instructions and follow-up questions. Lellee does not diagnose or change treatment instructions.
   - Review focus: health-navigation boundary; when professional/emergency care should supersede ordinary planning.

3. **I am worried about immediate safety**
   - Urgency: Emergency
   - Route: Emergency Care
   - Working guidance: If someone is in immediate danger or there is a medical emergency, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Review focus: emergency wording and whether any additional non-emergency alternative is required.

## Returning Home / Reentry — heightened review

4. **I do not have a safe place to stay**
   - Urgency: Urgent
   - Route: Resource Navigation
   - Working guidance: Prioritize immediate safe housing and basic needs, then organize follow-up steps.
   - Review focus: homelessness/safety routing; avoid implying guaranteed placement.

5. **I am worried about a requirement or deadline**
   - Urgency: Elevated
   - Route: In-app Guidance
   - Working guidance: Organize the requirement, date and questions. Lellee provides planning support, not individualized legal advice.
   - Review focus: legal-information boundary, court/probation/parole deadlines, and escalation language.

6. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Urgency: Emergency
   - Route: Emergency Care
   - Working guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Review focus: emergency wording.

## Housing Stability — heightened review

7. **I may lose my housing soon**
   - Urgency: Urgent
   - Route: Resource Navigation
   - Working guidance: Identify notices and deadlines, gather documents, and connect with qualified housing or legal resources as appropriate.
   - Review focus: legal-information boundary; eviction deadlines; no individualized legal advice.

8. **I have nowhere safe to stay**
   - Urgency: Urgent
   - Route: Resource Navigation
   - Working guidance: Prioritize immediate shelter and basic-needs resources, then organize longer-term housing follow-up.
   - Review focus: shelter/resource wording; avoid promising availability.

9. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Urgency: Emergency
   - Route: Emergency Care
   - Working guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Review focus: emergency wording.

## Building Independence — accessibility/privacy review

10. **An access barrier is stopping me**
   - Urgency: Elevated
   - Route: Resource Navigation
   - Working guidance: Identify the barrier and the accommodation, assistive technology, transportation, advocacy or community-living support that may help.
   - Review focus: disability-respectful language, autonomy, accessibility and accommodation boundaries.

11. **I need more or less support**
   - Urgency: Routine
   - Route: Support Contact
   - Working guidance: Review what you want to do yourself, where support is useful, and who you choose to involve.
   - Review focus: supported decision-making, autonomy, privacy, avoiding paternalistic framing.

12. **I am in immediate danger**
   - Urgency: Emergency
   - Route: Emergency Care
   - Working guidance: If you are in immediate danger, contact 911 or local emergency services now.
   - Review focus: emergency wording and accessibility considerations.

## Cross-program privacy checks for reviewer confirmation

Confirm that:
- cross-program sharing remains OFF by default;
- private Journal and private messages are not used by these routes unless explicitly shared;
- Trusted People access remains purpose-specific and revocable;
- program setup answers are user-entered and are not interpreted as diagnosis or eligibility findings;
- resource tools do not promise eligibility, housing placement, legal outcomes or clinical results;
- no agent independently diagnoses, prescribes, changes treatment, or makes emergency-disposition decisions.

## Accessibility checks

Confirm that the controlled beta should require:
- keyboard-operable controls and visible focus;
- meaningful labels for screen readers;
- no information conveyed by color alone;
- readable scaling at mobile zoom/text enlargement;
- plain-language alternatives for complex legal/medical terminology;
- user control over trusted-person involvement and shared information;
- no score that equates independence with doing everything without support.
