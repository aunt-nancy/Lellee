# Lellee Core + Programs + Coach Business

Lellee is the master brand.

Recovery is Program #1. The same platform can later power Reentry, Caregiving, Independent Living, Housing Stability, Grief, Workforce Reentry and other underserved programs.

## Coach Business
Coaching is a cross-program platform capability.

An approved individual can:
- create a coaching business;
- define 1-to-1, group and hybrid services;
- set prices for future payment activation;
- set group capacity;
- invite clients securely;
- coach multiple clients/groups;
- message clients;
- create assignments;
- schedule coaching sessions;
- track leads;
- operate under one Lellee account.

## Affordable group model
A coach can create a group with capacity (for example 6–12 people), then offer a lower per-person group price while using limited 1-to-1 touchpoints in a hybrid package.

## Privacy design
Coaches do NOT receive direct access to the client's journal, check-ins, recovery history or support contacts.
A separate coach_shared_items table contains only content the client explicitly elects to share.

## Business independence
The coach owns the coaching business relationship with clients subject to Lellee's platform terms.
Actual payment collection/payout is deliberately disabled until the marketplace/payout, tax, legal and processor configuration is designed.

## Review
Coach businesses begin as pending_review. Public marketplace listing is OFF.
