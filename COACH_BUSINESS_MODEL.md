# Lellee Coach Business Model

## Solo Coach
One individual can start and operate a coaching practice.

## Services
1-to-1:
- higher-touch individual support.

Group:
- one coach supports several people during the same session/program cycle;
- can reduce per-person price.

Hybrid:
- group coaching + limited individual check-ins;
- strongest affordability/scalability model.

## Dashboard
- Clients
- Groups
- Services & pricing
- Messages
- Assignments
- Leads
- Sessions (database foundation)

## Recommended future additions
- coach public profile/directory;
- consultation booking;
- payment/payout onboarding;
- coach subscription tier;
- client reviews with moderation;
- coach credential/document verification;
- templates for 6-, 8-, 12-week group programs;
- organization/team coaching accounts.

## Guardrails
Coaching must not be marketed as licensed treatment unless the coach/product relationship is separately credentialed and approved.
Emergency/safety needs always route outside ordinary coaching workflows.
