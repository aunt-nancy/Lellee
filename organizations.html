A SIMPLE OPERATING MODEL

Sponsor access. Organize participation. Review the big picture.

01

Create the organization

Submit a nonprofit, public agency, employer, school, healthcare, recovery, or community organization for Lellee review.

02

Request programs and seats

Request program licenses and seat allocations. A human administrator reviews activation instead of automatically opening programs.

03

Invite participants

Send private sponsored-access links tied to the invited email address and an approved program license.

04

Use cohorts when useful

Organize sponsored participants by program, location, site, grant, funding source or operating group.

05

Review aggregate operations

See seat assignment, activation and program-level totals without turning Lellee into an employee or client surveillance system.

06

Keep roles separate

Organization workspaces, coaching workspaces and personal Lellee data remain distinct even when one person has more than one role.

PRIVACY IS STRUCTURAL

Sponsoring access does not buy access to a person’s private Lellee life.

The organization side is designed around seat management and aggregate operations. Participants retain control of their personal workspace and private content.

Organization can manage

Program licenses, seats, invitations, cohorts, team members and approved aggregate operations.

Organization does not automatically see

Journals, private check-ins, coaching messages, support contacts, safety selections, diagnoses or individual recovery history.

Small-group protection

Advanced reporting should suppress or limit outputs when groups are too small to aggregate safely.

Human review

Organization and program-license approval stay deliberate rather than automatically opening access.

ORGANIZATION OPERATIONS

The workspace is designed to grow beyond basic seat management.

The current Lellee app already has dedicated destinations for analytics, licensing/revenue preparation, outreach drafts, automation, integrations, forms and quick-start setup. Those remain separate from participant private content.
