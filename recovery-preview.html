RECOVERY · YOUR JOURNEY

Your Recovery Journey

Lellee organizes the first year into manageable stages while keeping long-term recovery available beyond that.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilize

Safety, routine, cravings and immediate support.

DAYS 31–60

Build

Triggers, coping skills, support and daily recovery practices.

DAYS 61–90

Understand

Patterns, thoughts, behaviors, relationships and accountability.

MONTHS 4–6

Rebuild, Strengthen & Grow

Daily life, relapse prevention, identity, values and long-term goals.

MONTHS 7–12

Maintenance

Maintain gains, handle setbacks and keep strengthening connection.

YEAR 2+

Long-Term Recovery

Continue building a meaningful life in recovery.

Your personal place in the journey is private.

Create an account when you want Lellee to remember your recovery date, progress, preferences or completed work.

RECOVERY · TOOLS

Recovery Tools

Browse simple skills first. Saving a personalized tool plan requires an account.

GROUNDING

5-4-3-2-1

Slow down and notice five things you see, four you can feel, three you hear, two you smell and one you taste.

PAUSE

HALT Check

Ask whether you are hungry, angry, lonely or tired before making the next decision.

URGES

Delay the Decision

Create time between an urge and an action. Change location, contact support or choose a short competing activity.

SUPPORT

Keep a short list of people or services you can contact when recovery feels harder.
