Recovery Residence vs. Sober Living

Terms vary by location, but recovery residences generally describe alcohol- and drug-free living environments intended to support recovery outside formal inpatient treatment.

Lellee Editorial Team · Educational recovery content

Start with the person, not the rule

Recovery is highly personal. Use this information as a way to understand options and make a safer next decision, not as a test of whether recovery is being done the “right” way.

Terminology is inconsistent

Sober living, sober home and recovery residence may be used differently across programs, states and communities.

Housing is not automatically treatment

A recovery residence may provide structure and peer support without being licensed to provide clinical substance-use treatment.

Verify before choosing

Ask about rules, fees, staffing, medication policies, house expectations, accreditation or certification where relevant, and what services are actually provided.

Common questions

Does everyone need the same recovery approach?

No. Recovery can involve different combinations of professional care, peer support, mutual-help, medication, skills, faith or spirituality, and personal supports.

Can self-guided recovery tools replace treatment?

No. Self-guided tools can support recovery but are not substitutes for needed medical, behavioral-health, withdrawal, or emergency care.
