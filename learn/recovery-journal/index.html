Common questions

Is recovery the same for everyone?

No. Recovery pathways, treatment needs, health needs, and support systems differ.

When should I seek professional help?

Seek professional support when a concern is beyond self-guided support, involves medical or withdrawal concerns, or raises immediate safety concerns.
