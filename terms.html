Terms of Use

Terms for using the consumer version of Lellee.

Qualified legal review required before public launch.

This is a counsel-review candidate built around Lellee's current consumer architecture and current official privacy guidance. It is not a substitute for advice from an attorney who has reviewed Lellee's entity structure, contracts, vendors, states served, and actual data flows.

Counsel-review version: 2026-08-10 · Effective date intentionally blank until approval.

1. Eligibility

The recommended consumer launch is for adults age 18 and older. A youth version requires separate legal and product review.

2. The service

Lellee provides recovery-oriented education, guided reflection, journaling, planning, coping tools, reminders, resource discovery, support-network organization, milestones and long-term recovery tracking.

3. Not clinical or emergency care

Lellee is not an emergency service, detoxification or withdrawal-management service, licensed substance-use treatment program, therapist, doctor, pharmacy or crisis-response service. Lellee does not diagnose a substance-use disorder, determine a clinical level of care, prescribe medication or guarantee recovery outcomes.

4. Provider use is separate

Treatment programs, clinicians, recovery residences, coaches and organizations should not use the consumer account system as a clinical record or multi-client provider dashboard unless Lellee separately approves that professional product under appropriate contracts, roles, privacy controls and regulatory review.

5. Accounts

You are responsible for protecting your login credentials and for activity through your account. Do not use Lellee to access or disclose another person's private recovery information without authority.

6. Your content

You retain ownership of original journal entries and reflections you submit. You give Lellee the limited permission necessary to host, process, back up and display that content to you and to perform features you request. Storing a journal does not give Lellee permission to publish it publicly.

7. Recovery Story

Recovery Story is user-directed. Review selected content before exporting, printing or sharing it.

8. Resources and sponsorship

Resource information can change and should be verified directly. Listings are not guarantees or clinical determinations. Sponsored placements must be labeled; payment does not alter emergency guidance or establish that a resource is clinically appropriate.

9. Paid services

Lellee may offer optional Plus features or physical products. Before a charge, price and billing interval should be presented clearly. Core safety and essential recovery-support functions are not intended to depend on buying Plus.

10. Acceptable use

Do not misuse Lellee to attack the service, access another account, upload malicious code, violate law, impersonate another person, exploit sensitive recovery information, or facilitate illegal drug sales or other harmful conduct.

11. No guarantee

Recovery is individual. Lellee does not guarantee abstinence, prevention of return to use, medical outcomes, employment, housing, legal outcomes or any other result.

12. Disputes

This counsel-review candidate intentionally does not insert mandatory arbitration, class-action waiver, governing-law or venue clauses without qualified legal review.

Lellee — private, practical recovery support for today and the long view. The consumer service is not an emergency service, licensed treatment program, or substitute for individualized medical or behavioral-health care.
