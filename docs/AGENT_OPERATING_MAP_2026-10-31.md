# Lellee agent operating map

October 31, 2026

This review covers the 18 application specialists. All 18 retain required human review and no autonomous external action. Existing product, pricing, program-activation and release approvals are preserved.

## Findings and repair

- All 18 specialists now have a team, explicit inputs, a required deliverable, and a next owner.
- The professional-training specialist is assigned to Build & Quality; its new event template is inactive pending approved mapping and availability triggers. Five previously cancelled training tasks were not requeued.
- The Program Builder no longer plans against an obsolete seven-program limit. It must consider the full approved portfolio while preserving Recovery and existing activation decisions.
- The database holds Recovery plus 20 additional program records. The user describes 20 programs; this difference is flagged for reconciliation, with no records added, renamed, activated, or removed.
- Before repair there were 44 pending outputs and zero handoff records. This repair does not mark those outputs approved or delete them.
- The old mission helper only created the first task. The new worker checks two recurring missions: demand intelligence and operating coordination.
- New recurring work is capped at two assignments per Los Angeles calendar day. Only one current assignment per mission may remain outstanding. A pending review, failed run, or blocked run pauses that mission; a missing human owner prevents queuing.
- Other specialists run on specific supplied inputs or approved handoffs, rather than generating empty recurring analysis. Daily and weekly labels express eligibility/review rhythm, not a guarantee of a new model run.
- Trend, resource-freshness and explicitly research-required tasks now receive required web-search access. Search execution and source presence are checked; demand briefs require at least three cited source URLs. Source quality and claims still require review.

## Recommendation and publishing workflow

Public demand and research -> Trend & Demand Intelligence draft -> Operations Copilot decision brief -> owner approval -> Program Builder and publishing outline -> content and accessibility review -> pricing and marketing proposals -> owner publication/release approval.

Each opportunity brief must identify the evidence, limitations, overlap with existing journeys, whether it should be a module or a new journey, optional book and workbook, responsible next specialist, and the exact decision needed. Stress and Overwhelm and Sleep and Rest are initial assessment candidates, not approved or published journeys.

## Full roster

### Growth & Revenue

#### Coach Business Growth Agent

Independent-coach business development.

**Inputs:** Approved independent-coaching business tools and supplied non-sensitive business metadata.

**Deliverable:** Service and group-program proposals, capacity planning, onboarding improvements, and optional approved companion materials; no clinical judgments.

**Next owner:** Coach Quality and Operations Copilot; Growth & Marketing after approval.

**Trigger:** Weekly with supplied business inputs.

#### Growth & Marketing Agent

Consumer positioning and acquisition strategy.

**Inputs:** Approved opportunity brief, verified product facts, approved offers, and supplied aggregate performance.

**Deliverable:** Messaging, SEO and content themes, campaign concepts, and proposed conversion tests; link journey, book, and workbook when approved.

**Next owner:** Operations Copilot for approval; Social Media Growth receives approved messaging.

**Trigger:** Weekly when approved inputs change.

#### Prospect Research Agent

Organization and distribution partner discovery.

**Inputs:** Approved target types and public organization information.

**Deliverable:** Ranked public-business partner, sponsor, employer, nonprofit, or distribution prospects with sources and uncertainty.

**Next owner:** Growth & Marketing and Operations Copilot; contact requires owner authorization.

**Trigger:** Weekly with an approved target brief.

#### Revenue Intelligence Agent

Commercial viability and pricing scenarios.

**Inputs:** Approved product scope, current authorized pricing, public comparables, and supplied aggregate revenue and cost data.

**Deliverable:** Assumption-labelled pricing and packaging options, production cost considerations, and journey-book-workbook economics; do not change approved prices.

**Next owner:** Operations Copilot for the owner decision; Growth & Marketing after approval.

**Trigger:** Weekly when relevant inputs change.

#### Social Media Growth Agent

Social content execution drafts.

**Inputs:** Approved messaging and supplied aggregate platform performance.

**Deliverable:** Platform-specific draft posts and calendar; consumer and independent-coach tracks kept distinct; no automatic posting.

**Next owner:** Growth & Marketing, then owner approval for each external action.

**Trigger:** Approved campaign or changed performance; daily input review.

#### Trend & Demand Intelligence Agent

Public demand, emerging needs, and journey and publishing opportunity discovery.

**Inputs:** Current public demand signals, published research, full approved portfolio, and non-sensitive publishing research summaries.

**Deliverable:** Ranked opportunity brief distinguishing measured demand, health need, research evidence, and untested ideas; compare existing journey, module, new journey, book, and companion workbook.

**Next owner:** Operations Copilot for a decision brief; after owner approval, Program Builder Assistant.

**Trigger:** Daily eligibility check; a pending current review pauses the next run.

### Build & Quality

#### Accessibility QA Agent

Accessibility and practical usability.

**Inputs:** Supplied interface, document, or workbook version and relevant test results.

**Deliverable:** Specific findings about keyboard and screen-reader use, headings, contrast, text resizing, motion, accessible alternatives, and worksheet writing space.

**Next owner:** Content QA or implementation owner; Release QA tracks resolution.

**Trigger:** New or revised surface; weekly review when inputs change.

#### Content QA Agent

Editorial quality, evidence wording, and companion-workbook consistency.

**Inputs:** Supplied versioned journey, book, training, or workbook draft with references.

**Deliverable:** Prioritized corrections, claim-to-source checks, duplication and reading-level findings, and consistency of exercises with their source; no claim of clinical validation.

**Next owner:** Program Builder or publishing editor for revisions; Release QA for readiness findings.

**Trigger:** New or revised supplied content; weekly review when inputs change.

#### Knowledge Assistant

Instructions and help after product decisions.

**Inputs:** Approved features, journey scopes, and changed user workflows.

**Deliverable:** Clear Help Center, FAQ, onboarding, and workbook-use instructions; distinguish drafted, available, and planned features.

**Next owner:** Content QA and Operations Copilot.

**Trigger:** Approved feature or workflow changes; weekly input review.

#### Program Builder Assistant

Architecture across the full approved Lellee portfolio.

**Inputs:** Approved opportunity brief and current program registry; identify conflicts between approved program count and database record count.

**Deliverable:** Journey or module proposal with audience, scope, sequence, practical exercises, accessibility, progress, companion-workbook outline, evidence gaps, dependencies, and release criteria.

**Next owner:** Content QA and Accessibility QA, then Operations Copilot for owner review.

**Trigger:** After an approved opportunity or a supplied program configuration change.

#### Release QA Agent

Release acceptance evidence and unresolved gates.

**Inputs:** Actual QA results, proposed scope, approvals, and unresolved defects.

**Deliverable:** Passed, failed, not-run, and waiting-for-approval checks with evidence; no invented pass and no release approval.

**Next owner:** Operations Copilot for a concise owner decision.

**Trigger:** Daily eligibility review of changed release evidence; no empty rerun.

#### Professional Training Evidence Builder

Professional training evidence and assessments.

**Inputs:** Participant-availability event and an explicitly approved training and certification mapping.

**Deliverable:** Sourced professional-training draft, objectives, applied assessments, mixed findings, and scope limits; no duplication of the consumer workbook.

**Next owner:** Content QA and Release QA, through Operations Copilot.

**Trigger:** Event-driven; remain unqueued until the training mapping and triggering event are approved.

### Operations & Success

#### Coach Quality Agent

Independent-coach operational quality.

**Inputs:** Approved credential and disclosure state, business capacity, and supplied aggregate feedback.

**Deliverable:** Operational improvements and unresolved qualification gates; no clinical outcome ranking or automatic discipline.

**Next owner:** Coach Business Growth and Operations Copilot.

**Trigger:** Weekly or supplied material operational change.

#### Customer Success Agent

Organization rollout, adoption, and recurring product friction.

**Inputs:** Approved organization implementation records and supplied aggregate adoption metadata.

**Deliverable:** Milestones, training needs, adoption risks, and unmet practical needs; never infer individual health conditions.

**Next owner:** Support Triage, Knowledge Assistant, and Operations Copilot.

**Trigger:** Weekly or an explicitly supplied organization event.

#### Operations Copilot

Coordination, missing inputs, review backlog, and handoff accountability.

**Inputs:** Non-private roster, mission, task, output-review and handoff counts plus supplied approved project facts.

**Deliverable:** One concise operating brief naming each blocker, responsible specialist, missing input, next action, and at most three essential owner decisions; identify stale work without approving it.

**Next owner:** Relevant specialist for internal preparation; Leslie Hollis for essential approvals.

**Trigger:** Daily eligibility check with a real aggregate snapshot; pending current review pauses next run.

#### Support Triage Agent

Support routing and recurring friction.

**Inputs:** Only support tickets explicitly supplied to the task; aggregate patterns must not identify participants.

**Deliverable:** Recommended support routing, severity, repeated friction, and a de-identified product-needs summary.

**Next owner:** Customer Success and Knowledge Assistant; aggregate opportunity suggestions to Trend & Demand through Operations Copilot.

**Trigger:** A supplied ticket or aggregate support pattern; no unrelated private access.

### Research & Resources

#### Provider Research Agent

Initial public provider and service research.

**Inputs:** A specific supplied provider or resource research queue.

**Deliverable:** Sourced public identity, services, licensing references where relevant, and unresolved verification questions.

**Next owner:** Human resource verifier; Resource Freshness handles subsequent change checks.

**Trigger:** New or changed supplied provider queue; weekly input review.

#### Resource Freshness Agent

Changes to already-listed resources and provider information.

**Inputs:** A specific supplied resource list, prior source dates, and public pages.

**Deliverable:** Changed links, hours, services, availability, and dates; mark discrepancies for human reverification.

**Next owner:** Human resource verifier; Provider Research for unresolved initial facts.

**Trigger:** Weekly when a defined resource queue is supplied; urgent public changes as surfaced.
