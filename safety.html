Safety

Lellee is a recovery support tool and is not an emergency service. For overdose symptoms, severe breathing problems, loss of consciousness, severe illness, or another medical emergency, seek emergency medical care.

U.S. crisis support

If you are in suicidal crisis or emotional distress in the United States or its territories, call or text 988.

Return to use

Respond first to immediate safety: get to a safer place, avoid driving, contact trusted support, and seek urgent medical care when medically unsafe. After immediate safety, reconnect with treatment or recovery support.
