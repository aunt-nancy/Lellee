Medical emergency or suspected overdose

For loss of consciousness, severe breathing problems, suspected overdose, seizure, severe illness, or another medical emergency, call 911 or seek emergency medical care. Do not rely on Lellee to monitor an emergency.

Suicidal crisis or emotional distress

In the United States and its territories, call or text 988 for the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate physical danger, call 911.

Withdrawal

Withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines and some other substances can become medically dangerous. Lellee does not determine whether withdrawal is safe to manage without medical care. Seek qualified medical evaluation when withdrawal may be serious or uncertain.

Return to substance use

Address immediate physical safety first. Avoid driving or other dangerous activity, move to a safer setting when possible, and seek emergency or medical care when the situation may be medically unsafe. After immediate safety, reconnect with appropriate treatment, recovery support or a trusted support person.

Help Me Right Now

Lellee's Help Me Right Now tools are designed to create a pause, suggest coping or connection options and make support easier to reach. They are not a substitute for emergency, crisis, medical or professional treatment.

Notifications

Push notifications are convenience features. Delivery may be delayed or fail because of browser, device, network or service conditions. Never depend on a Lellee notification for medication safety, emergency response, court deadlines or another critical obligation.

Resource listings

Confirm availability, hours, eligibility, cost and services directly with the resource. Sponsored placement is not a clinical recommendation.
