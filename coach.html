PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

Coaching tools are not automatically clinical treatment.

Accurate credentials

Coaches must describe credentials, training and scope accurately.

Client-controlled sharing

Clients decide which appropriate Lellee information they share with a coach.

No diagnosis by default

The coaching product does not turn ordinary coaching into medical care, psychotherapy or emergency services.

Human accountability

Business and AI tools can prepare work, but sensitive decisions remain with people.
